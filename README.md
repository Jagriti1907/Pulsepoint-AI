# PulsePoint AI 🚀

Turn long-form videos into viral short-form content using GenAI.

---

## 🔍 Problem Statement

Mentors, educators, and creators produce hours of high-value long-form
content such as lectures, podcasts, and workshops. However, modern
audiences consume content in short 30–60 second bursts.

As a result, valuable “golden nuggets” of emotional or impactful insights
often remain hidden inside long videos and are difficult to identify
manually.

---

## 💡 Solution – PulsePoint AI

PulsePoint AI is a GenAI-powered prototype that helps creators quickly
surface viral-worthy moments from long videos.

The platform allows users to:
- Upload a long-form video
- Automatically identify high-impact moments
- Generate timestamps and captions suitable for short-form platforms

This transforms long sessions into snackable, shareable content.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works (Prototype Flow)

1. User uploads a long-form video (MP4)
2. The system simulates AI-based emotional peak detection
3. A viral-worthy timestamp is surfaced
4. A high-engagement caption and explanation are generated

This prototype demonstrates the complete end-to-end workflow.
